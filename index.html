How it works

1. Design it

Pick a template or a blank board. Colours, fades and wood stains, patterns, 35 fonts, curved text, clip art and your own pictures. What you see is what we print.

2. We build it

Regulation 24" x 48" boards, 3/4" Baltic birch tops on a solid frame, folding legs, 6" hole 9" from the top. Your design is printed and sealed under a clear coat - or a matte guest-book finish your wedding guests can sign.

3. It ships

Built to order in about three weeks (five business days with the rush option) and shipped freight to your door. Tell us the date you need it by and we plan around it.
