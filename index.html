Help

Edit boards: Choose matching boards or select Board A or B. Drag an item to move it; use its square handle to resize and round handle to rotate.

Keyboard: Focus the board, then use arrow keys to move the selection, + or - to resize, and Delete to remove it.

Text: Replace a template's words in the Text tab; Enter starts a new line. Auto-fit keeps the words within their original space; manual sizing switches it off.

Pictures: Add PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG files. Transparent PNGs work well for logos. Use Remove white background for a logo on white; full-size originals are kept for printing. Fill the board puts one picture edge to edge behind everything, trimmed to fit; select it from an empty spot on the board or its row in the Art list, and untick it to put it back.

Files: Save editable copy keeps a project you can reopen. Share preview creates an image; Print file downloads full-size artwork.

Two tabs: This browser keeps one design, saved after every change. If it is open in two tabs, the last change saved wins; the other tab says so and offers Reload.
